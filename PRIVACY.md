# Autobahn Rush: privacy policy

Autobahn Rush does not collect, send or share any personal data.

- No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- Your settings, best score and removed tracks are stored only on your device
  (browser storage).
- Music files you add in the MUSIC menu are stored only on your device and are
  never uploaded.
- Tilt steering reads the phone's motion sensor on the device, only while you
  play. Nothing is recorded or sent.
- Vibration is used for crash feedback and can be switched off in Settings.

Contact: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
