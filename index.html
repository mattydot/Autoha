HOW TO DRIVE

Steer with the ◀ ▶ buttons (or tilt the phone, see Settings). Gas is automatic; hold BRAKE to slow down. On a keyboard: W A S D.

2 / 3

TRAFFIC & POINTS

Speed and close passes score points. Flash your lights (✦ or F) and slow cars move over. Take an Ausfahrt to explore country roads, villages and off-road tracks. Money from each run unlocks new cars.

3 / 3

THE POLIZEI

Hit a car and drive on, or race past a speed camera, and a patrol car comes. Follow it when it shows BITTE FOLGEN and stop behind it: just a fine. Run, and it becomes a chase with a helicopter, SEK vans and spike strips.
