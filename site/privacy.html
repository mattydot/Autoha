Autobahn Rush: privacy policy

Autobahn Rush itself does not collect personal data: there are no accounts and no analytics of our own. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob does process data, as described below.

Ads (Android app)

  • The app shows a full-screen ad between runs (every third run), and an optional rewarded video ad when you choose "keep going" after a crash or an arrest.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Datenschutz & Werbung.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

The web version shows no ads and loads nothing from ad networks.

On your device only

  • Your settings, best score and removed tracks are stored only on your device.
  • Music files you add in the MUSIC menu are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Tilt steering reads the phone's motion sensor on the device, only while you play. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Vibration is used for crash feedback and can be switched off in Settings.
